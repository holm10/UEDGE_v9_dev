## 1. Executive Summary

UEDGE v9 can evaluate the right-hand side (RHS) and the finite-difference Jacobian in parallel with OpenMP (`ppp/`,
switches `ppp.OMPParallelPandf1` and `ppp.OMPParallelJac`). The epic owner sees **run-to-run differences** with OpenMP on.
When the same case runs in separate Python sessions with the same thread count, the Newton residual norm `fnrm` printed
by `nksol` differs, while the RHS vector `yldot` does not obviously differ. Turning equations off one at a time showed
that the effect does **not** come from the ion continuity or parallel momentum equations and **is** present in the
electron energy equation. The ion energy and potential equations had not been checked.

A short characterisation during planning, with the owner's case (§6.3), suggests the problem goes beyond noise:
- **The OpenMP Jacobian disagrees with serial in the electron-energy (Te) rows.** Te diagonal entries reached about
  −2.6·10⁴³ where serial gives about −9·10⁴, and the sparsity pattern changed from run to run.
- **The disagreement depends on threading.** With 1 thread, the OpenMP Jacobian was bitwise equal to serial. With 4
  threads it was not, even with one chunk per thread.
- **The other equations look clean, on the Jacobian-only arm.** Ion energy, ion continuity, parallel momentum and
  neutral gas, each run alone at 4 threads, were bitwise equal to serial.
- **The OpenMP RHS also disagreed with serial** in the Te rows, once it was active.

These are planning observations, not conclusions. Phase 1 confirms them.

A solver that takes a different, and partly wrong, Newton path on every run cannot be regression-tested, profiled or
trusted. CI builds with `--serial` only (`.claude/context/testing.md`), so none of this is caught.

**MVP goal (Jacobian track).**
- With the OpenMP build, `OMPParallelJac=1` and `OMPParallelPandf1=0`, the reproducer's Jacobian and `fnrm` sequence are
  **bitwise equal to the serial run, in every fresh session and at every tested thread count**. If Phase 2 shows
  bitwise equality is unreachable, the owner picks the fallback gate defined in §11 (decision 9).
- Serial results and every default stay **bitwise unchanged**.
- A new CI job builds UEDGE with OpenMP and fails if Jacobian agreement breaks again.

**Bonus (Jacobian + RHS track).** The same agreement with `OMPParallelPandf1=1`, in separate tickets, if it can be
resolved (decision 8).

## 2. Mission

Make UEDGE's OpenMP Jacobian give the serial answer, reproducibly, and keep it that way, without changing a single
default result. Then do the same for the OpenMP RHS if possible.

**Core principles**
1. **Nothing may change.** The defaults are serial (`OMPParallelJac=0`, `OMPParallelPandf1=0`, `ppp/ppp.v:6-7`). Serial
   results must stay bitwise identical, in both the `--serial` build and the default OpenMP build with both switches
   off. No default value of any switch changes (decisions 4 and 7).
2. **Serial is the reference for OpenMP.** The gate is bitwise equality with serial. It is deterministic, so the
   stable-but-wrong case (§6.3) cannot pass it. Whether bitwise equality is reachable for Te is a Phase 2 exit question
   for the owner (decision 9).
3. **Jacobian first.** The Jacobian track gates the epic. The RHS track is a bonus, in its own tickets, and depends on
   the Jacobian track (decision 8).
4. **Reproduce first, then explain, then fix.** No fix is written before a root-cause document explains it.
5. **The tickets design the test flow.** This PRD fixes what must be checked (§7) and how success is judged (§11).
6. **Guard the fix in CI.** The agreement check runs on every PR.

## 6. Core Architecture & Patterns

### 6.1 How the OpenMP path works (facts the work starts from)
- **Parallel Jacobian.**
  - `jac_calc_parallel` (`ppp/parallel.F90:45-156`) calls `jac_calc_omp` → `OMPJacBuilder`
    (`ppp/omp_parallel.F90:495`, `:645`).
  - Before the loop, the master evaluates the full RHS at `t=0.0` and copies the `+threadprivate` arrays and scalars to
    every thread (`ppp/omp_parallel.F90:679-692`).
  - The column loop is `!$omp parallel do schedule(dynamic,OMPLoopJacNchunk)` (`:712-715`).
- **Chunking.**
  - `OMPJacNchunks=0` (default, `ppp/ppp.v:43`) gives `NchunksJac=neq` (`ppp/omp_parallel.F90:313`). `OMPJacNchunks<0`
    gives `NchunksJac=Nthreads`, but the loop stays `schedule(dynamic)`, so one thread may still take two chunks.
  - `OMPAutoBalance=1` (default) rescales the chunk weights from measured times (`:548-559`). The timings are indexed by
    thread, not chunk (`:734`).
- **Thread count.** `OMP_NUM_THREADS` sets the team size. `ppp.Nthreads` is clamped to `OMP_GET_MAX_THREADS()`
  (`:294-310`).
- **Serial and parallel Jacobian differ structurally:**
  - `LocalJacBuilder` makes an extra reset `pandf` call when `isjacreset>=1` and `mod(iv,numvar)==0`
    (`ppp/parallel.F90:320-325`). Serial has that call commented out (`bbb/oderhs.m:1220-1222`).
  - Clipping uses `sfscal(iv)` in parallel and `sfscal(ii)` in serial.
  - The perturbation is `delperturb` in parallel and `del` in serial.
  - The parallel path always adds `1/dtuse` and `nufak`.
  - `ijactot` is incremented at a different point.
  - `LocalJacBuilder`'s automatic `wk` and `yl_check` arrays (`ppp/parallel.F90:195-196`) are only partly initialised.
- **Parallel RHS.**
  - `OMPPandf1Rhs` (`ppp/omp_parallel.F90:902-996`) runs in parallel only when `ijactot>0`, and falls back to serial
    `pandf` otherwise (`:949`, `:989-991`).
  - `exmain` resets `ijactot=0` (`bbb/odesetup.m:6799`). In `sfsetnk`, `yldot0` is therefore evaluated serially
    (`bbb/oderhs.m:2327`), and the first Jacobian then sets `ijactot=1`. From there on, every `nksol` residual goes
    through the parallel RHS, including the iteration-0 `f(u0)`, line searches and Krylov difference-quotient products
    (`mfdif=2`, `svr/nksol.m:865`). The call chain is `rhsnk` → `PandfRHS_interface` → `OMPPandf1Rhs`
    (`bbb/pandf.m:19-21`).
  - `OMPParallelPandf1=1` forces `OMPParallelJac=1` (`ppp/parallel.F90:30-36`). The only two arms are therefore
    Jacobian-only, and Jacobian + RHS.
- **The parallel RHS also differs structurally from serial:**
  - it evaluates padded subdomain chunks, including dedicated core-ring chunks (`ppp/omp_parallel.F90:64-84`);
  - it widens an `auxrange`, with the comment "TODO: Figure out why this is needed??" (`:1015-1030`);
  - it calls `bouncon` only for chunks at a domain edge (`:1093-1105`);
  - each chunk runs on per-thread state, with no refresh from the master before the RHS loop;
  - each `yldot` index is summed from the one chunk that owns it (`REDUCTION(+)`, exact when ownership is disjoint).

  The reproducer uses non-local boundary conditions, such as core ring sums for `iflcore=1` and `isnicore=3`. Whether
  these come out exact depends on this decomposition. That is one reason the RHS is the less robust track.
- **The iteration-0 `fnrm` depends on the Jacobian.** With `issfon=1`, `sfsetnk` (`bbb/oderhs.m:2292`) builds `sfscal`
  from the row norms (`rnrms`, `normtype`) of the freshly computed, `suscal`-scaled Jacobian. So `fnrm = ‖yldot·sfscal‖`
  changes when the Jacobian changes, even with bitwise-identical `yldot`. This explains the owner's observation.
  `nksol` prints `fnrm` with `g26.16` (`svr/nksol.m:1100`).
- **What `bbb.jac` holds after `exmain`.** It is not the raw finite-difference Jacobian. It has been:
  - column-scaled by `1/suscal` (`bbb/oderhs.m:2342`, `:1955`);
  - row-normalised in `psetnk` (`:1963`);
  - permuted in place by reverse Cuthill–McKee when `ireorder=1`, the default (`bbb/bbb.v:2975`;
    `bbb/oderhs.m:1308`, `:1402-1427`). The permutation depends on the sparsity pattern.
- **Existing checks.**
  - `CheckJac` and `ForceSerialCheck` use an absolute tolerance of `1e-14` and abort on the first mismatch
    (`ppp/parallel.F90:84-150`). `CheckPandf1` uses absolute and relative `1e-14` (`:354-356`).
  - They change solver state. `ForceSerialCheck` replaces the solver's Jacobian with the serial one, so it is not a
    baseline.

### 6.3 Preliminary evidence (planning, 2026-10-14; Phase 1 confirms and extends it)
**Setup.**
- macOS arm64, Homebrew gcc-15 (gfortran and libgomp), 14 cores.
- Two builds: current `v9-develop` (`0fac655`) and an April 2026 v9 beta.
- Each sample was a fresh `python -I` process with `OMP_NUM_THREADS=Nthreads`, `OMP_STACKSIZE=512M`, and the rate
  directories set.
- Each sample ran the deck, then `exmain` with `ftol=1e20`, which means one Jacobian in `sfsetnk` and the iteration-0
  `fnrm`.
- The Jacobians compared were the post-`exmain` `bbb.jac` arrays (§6.1), compared run against serial and run against
  run.
- "Same" means bitwise-equal float64.
- The raw data is not kept. Phase 1 re-measures it.

| Configuration (Jacobian-only arm unless noted) | neq | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| Owner's Te box, 4 threads | 25 | **`fnrm` differed in 10/10 runs** (0.146–0.185; serial 0.322). The sparsity pattern differed every run. `sfscal` differed in 4–19 of 25 rows. `yldot` was bitwise identical. Te diagonal entries reached **≈ −2.6·10⁴³** (serial ≈ −9·10⁴), and some off-diagonal entries equalled another column's diagonal exactly. Both builds behaved the same. |
| Te box, serial, repeated | 25 | Bitwise identical. |
| Te box, OpenMP with **1 thread** | 25 | Bitwise equal to serial. A single thread works through the columns in serial order from the master's state, so this reproduces serial's history and does **not** show that N threads can match serial. |
| Te box, 4 threads, `OMPJacNchunks=-1`, `OMPAutoBalance=0` | 25 | Still non-deterministic: 3 distinct `fnrm` in 4 runs. The loop is still `schedule(dynamic)`, so the chunk-to-thread mapping can vary. This is consistent with a race, and also with uninitialised or incompletely copied worker-thread state. |
| Te box, 4 threads, with individual physics off (H radiation, Joule, E×B, grad-B, flux limit) | 25 | Non-deterministic every time. |
| Te only, whole grid, 4 threads | 3168 | Non-deterministic in 3/3 runs, with a different sparsity pattern each time. |
| Ti only / ni only / up only / ng only, 4 threads | 3168 / 25344 / 6336 / 3168 | Bitwise equal to serial and reproducible (3 runs each). This is evidence for these equations on this arm only. |
| phi only | — | **Not a valid isolation.** Serial also aborts with "Jacobian row = 0 for eqn iv = 84". |
| Full problem, 4 and 8 threads | 44352 | The same `fnrm` in 7/7 runs (1.53541911164836…), but serial gives 1.91882062911127…. 2940 of the 3168 Te rows of `sfscal` differ from serial. Because `normtype=0` uses the max-norm, equal `fnrm` only shows each row's largest entry is stable. The run-to-run stability of the full Jacobian was not checked. |
| Full problem, **Jacobian + RHS arm**, 4 threads | 44352 | `yldot` evaluated through the solver path (`ffun` after `exmain`, parallel branch) **differs from serial in about 2200 Te rows**. `fnrm` varied in the 15th digit between runs. |
| Shared-state scan: Te box, serial, perturbed local `pandf` | 25 | No non-threadprivate `.v` floating-point array in `bbb`/`com`/`api` was written, net or transiently (sentinel test), and no non-threadprivate `.v` variable changed. Not scanned: scalars, integer arrays, `save`d locals, `ppp` module variables, and the coverage of the `OmpCopy*` copy. |

**Environment facts found:**
- **Stack.** The OpenMP Jacobian crashed with SIGBUS at the default thread stack. `OMP_STACKSIZE=512M` fixed it.
- **Rate files.** The deck needs `aph.aphdir`/`api.apidir` set, plus the rate files `ehr2.dat` and `C_rates.adas`,
  which the repo does not bundle. It also needs `mist.dat`, which is read from the working directory.
- **gcc-15 build.** gcc-15's default C23 mode fails to compile `bbb/exmain.c` (incompatible function-pointer types).
  `CFLAGS=-std=gnu17` works around it (R6).
- **Data size.** The case's data files total about 16 MB: `b0p1.hdf5` 11.7 MB, `mist.dat` 2.0 MB,
  `save_input_mod4.h5` 1.5 MB, `gridue.hdf5` 1.0 MB.
- **Run time.** About 2 s for the Te box and 12 s for the full problem, at 4 threads, **iteration 0 only**.

### 6.4 Hypotheses and leads for the RCA (not conclusions)
| # | Hypothesis / lead | Status after planning |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | **History-dependent per-thread state.** A local evaluation reads module state last written by another column (Jacobian) or chunk (RHS) on the same thread. | Open. Under dynamic scheduling it predicts run-to-run variation. That fits the Te box, but not the stable full-problem result. |
| H2 | **Race on shared state written during `pandf`.** | Open. Fits the 1-thread vs 4-thread contrast. `.v` floating-point arrays are cleared for the Te box. The remaining candidates are listed in the scan row. |
| H3 | **`save`d locals or variables changed after `DATA` initialisation** in routines called from `pandf`. | Not scanned. Known: `api/fmombal.m:70,368,1064`. |
| H4 | **Timing-dependent partitioning** (`OMPAutoBalance`), combined with H1. | Contributes variability when `NchunksJac<neq`. |
| H6 | **Uninitialised or incompletely copied worker-thread state**, such as threadprivate variables missing from `OmpCopy*`, or uninitialised automatic arrays (L2). | Open. Values around 10⁴³ look like uninitialised memory. This could explain a systematic, stable error in the full problem. |
| L1 | **The Te-only impurity-radiation sink** for `isimpon=7` (fixed fraction, `afracs`): its rate-table evaluation and work storage in `api/`. | Open. The only Te-specific physics not switched off in the planning runs. |
| L2 | **Automatic `wk`/`yl_check` arrays** in `LocalJacBuilder`, only partly initialised (`ppp/parallel.F90:195-196`). | Open. |
| L3 | **`OMPTimeLocalJac` indexed by thread, not chunk** (`ppp/omp_parallel.F90:734`), feeding the load balancing. | Open. Affects partitioning only. |
| R-H | **RHS arm specifics:** chunk decomposition and non-local boundary conditions, the `auxrange` widening, edge-only `bouncon` (§6.1). | Open, bonus track. Order-dependent summation is unlikely, because index ownership is disjoint. The 15th-digit `fnrm` variation is unexplained. |

### 6.6 Key patterns
- **Fresh process per repeat, in a prepared run directory.**
  - Forthon module state is global, and `.uedgerc.py` can change state at import.
  - Each repeat runs `python -I` with an empty `HOME`, in a temporary run directory that holds the case files. The deck
    reads `b0p1.hdf5`, `gridue.hdf5`, `save_input_mod4.h5` and `mist.dat` by relative path.
  - `python -I` leaves the script's folder off `sys.path`, so the harness adds the case directory explicitly before
    importing the deck.
- **Pinned threads and stack.** Set `OMP_NUM_THREADS=N`, `ppp.Nthreads=N` and `OMP_STACKSIZE`, and record all three.
- **Edit sources only.** Fixes go in `.m`, `.v` and `.F90`. Never edit the generated `.F` files. MPPL hazards apply.
- **Threadprivate rule.** Any module variable assigned during RHS evaluation is `+threadprivate`.

- **Rate files.** `ehr2.dat` and `C_rates.adas` are not in the repo. The ticket decides where the test flow gets them:
  an environment variable plus a documented source, or a pinned download in CI. The deck must not need user-specific
  edits.

### 7.2 OpenMP test flow (requirements; the design is a ticket)
1. **Capture points.**
   - **Jacobian:** the unscaled, unpermuted CSR Jacobian as it comes out of `jac_calc` or `jac_calc_parallel`, before
     `suscal` scaling, row normalisation and RCM reordering. Alternatively, the post-`exmain` arrays with the
     permutation inverted.
   - **`sfscal`** and the **`fnrm` sequence**, both as float64 and as the 16 printed digits, over **≥ 3 Newton
     iterations**, as in `testscript2.py`.
   - **RHS arm:** `yldot` from every measured parallel evaluation, each with a check that the parallel branch actually
     ran.
2. **Agreement with serial.**
   - For each configuration and arm, at 2, 4 and 8 threads, compare with a serial run of the same build.
   - **Bitwise means equal IEEE-754 bit patterns.** `+0.0` and `−0.0` count as different, and any NaN or Inf in either
     result is a failure.
   - The sparsity pattern must match too.
   - This is the primary pass/fail.
3. **Determinism.** Run K fresh-process repeats per configuration, arm and thread count, and compare them bitwise.
   Acceptance sizes are in §11.
4. **Configurations:**
   - the owner's Te box;
   - the full problem;
   - each equation group alone: Te, Ti, ni, up, ng;
   - a valid isolation that includes phi, for example the full problem with `isteon=0`, or phi with ni and up
     (phi alone fails in serial);
   - each on both arms.
5. **Localise mismatches.** Map each mismatching entry, in unpermuted indexing, through `igyl` and the `idx*` arrays
   (`bbb/bbb.v:2002-2008`) to (equation, species, `ix`, `iy`), for both row and column. Report counts per equation pair
   and the first entries.
6. **Exit codes:** 0 when everything agrees and reproduces, 1 on a mismatch, 2 on a harness error. The Jacobian-only arm
   and the RHS arm get **separate verdicts**, so CI can gate on one and only report the other. Results are
   machine-readable, with provenance: commit, compiler and flags, `OMP_NUM_THREADS`, `Nthreads`, `OMP_STACKSIZE`,
   `OMPJacNchunks`, `OMPAutoBalance`, `OMPLoopJacNchunk`.
7. **Environment** as in §6.6. No `uetools`. Diagnostic switches (`CheckJac`, `CheckPandf1`, `ForceSerialCheck`) run
   only in separate diagnostic runs.

### 7.3 Serial old-vs-new bitwise check
- **Environments:** the base build (`v9-develop`) and the PR build, each in its own venv.
- **What it does:** runs a set of cases **serially** in both builds and compares bitwise (as defined in §7.2) the
  Jacobian at the §7.2 capture point, `yldot`, `sfscal` and the `fnrm` sequence. Exit codes as in §7.2.
- **Cases:**
  - the reproducer configurations from §7.2;
  - at least three coverage cases for shared `pandf` code: upstream `pytests` inputs (D_only, D+C, D_mols), a
    double-null case if one is available, or the AIUE-9 families once they exist.
- **Builds:** the `--serial` build, and the default OpenMP build with `OMPParallelJac=OMPParallelPandf1=0`.
- **Required** in every PR that touches Fortran, `.v` files or `ppp/`. `pytests` (`rtol=1e-5`) is no substitute.

### 7.4 Root-cause analysis (`docs/rca/AIUE-8-omp-jacobian-nondeterminism.md`)
- **Two sections:** Jacobian (gating) and RHS (bonus). Each has:
  - a characterisation table per configuration and thread count;
  - for each root cause: the variable or routine with `file:line`, the mechanism (from §6.4 or new), the evidence, the
    fix, and its effect on serial;
  - the refuted hypotheses.
- **The Jacobian section ends with the Phase 2 exit decision (decision 9).** Either bitwise agreement with serial is
  reachable for every tested configuration, or the owner chooses the fallback gate (§11) for the configurations where
  it isn't.

### 7.5 Optional in-code diagnostics
A ticket may add switches to `ppp/ppp.v`, for example:
- a repeat-and-compare Jacobian check;
- a deterministic amplifier, meaning a second evaluation under a fixed, different column-to-thread assignment;
- richer `CheckJac`/`ForceSerialCheck` mismatch reports.

Constraints: off by default; a units bracket and `#` description; existing pass/fail rules unchanged; §9 applies.

### 7.6 OpenMP CI job (`.github/workflows/build-test-omp.yml`)
- **Trigger:** push and pull_request.
- **Runner:** `ubuntu-latest` (4 vCPU), Python 3.11, `numpy==1.26.4`, `h5py`, and whatever else the test flow's README
  lists. It provides the rate files as §7.1 decides.
- **Build:** `pip install -v .`, the default OpenMP build.
- **Steps:**
  - the smoke test;
  - the §7.2 test flow on the Te box and the full problem, at 2 and 4 threads, with ≥ 3 Newton iterations and a serial
    reference per configuration;
  - artifacts uploaded.
- **Gating:** the Jacobian-only arm gates the job. The RHS arm runs and is reported, but does not gate until the RHS
  track lands its fix.
- **Detection is deterministic.** Before the fix, the full problem disagrees with serial on every run. Phase 4 measures
  20 CI runs on the pre-fix code and on a seeded race, and requires 20/20 failures.
- **Budget:** ≤ 20 min wall time, measured in Phase 4. The only cost data is about 12 s per full-problem run at
  iteration 0 on the planning machine. The real cost includes ≥ 3 iterations and the serial references, so Phase 4
  sets the repeat counts from measurement. If the budget can't be met, the full matrix runs nightly and the PR job runs
  a reduced set (R5).
- **Merge order:** the job merges together with, or after, the last Jacobian-fix ticket.

## 9. Physics & Numerics Impact

- **No new physics.** No spec in `docs/physics_specs/`. No equation, term or closure changes.
- **Default-results impact: none (refactor class).** After every ticket, the serial Jacobian, `yldot`, `sfscal` and the
  `fnrm` sequence must be **bitwise identical to `v9-develop`** in the §7.3 check. This applies to both the `--serial`
  build and the OpenMP build with both switches 0. No default switch value changes (decisions 4 and 7).
- **OpenMP results converge to serial.**
  - OpenMP quantities that disagree with serial today may change, but only towards bitwise equality with serial.
  - OpenMP quantities that equal serial today must keep doing so, for example the Ti, ni, up and ng isolations on the
    Jacobian arm.
  - OpenMP-only code changes that satisfy these rules are in scope without escalation, including chunking, resets and
    copy coverage (§4).
- **Escalation to the owner.** The work stops and the owner decides when:
  - (a) a root cause can only be removed by a change that moves serial results; or
  - (b) bitwise agreement with serial is unreachable on a tested configuration. This is the Phase 2 exit decision
    (decision 9): the owner picks the fallback gate in §11 for those configurations.

  For the RHS track, (b) may instead lead to recording a known limitation (§4).
- **Solver and OpenMP implications.**
  - A `+threadprivate` fix raises per-thread memory slightly.
  - Reset or copy fixes cost time. OpenMP Jacobian and RHS times are measured before and after, and a slowdown above
    10 % on the reproducer is reported to the owner.
  - No stencil widening and no change to `xlinc`/`xrinc`/`yinc`, because that would move serial results.

## 11. Success Criteria

**MVP success definition (Jacobian track, gating).**
- **Scope:** the reproducer, the OpenMP build, `OMPParallelJac=1`, `OMPParallelPandf1=0`, every configuration in §7.2,
  at 2, 4 and 8 threads.
- **Requirement:** the Jacobian, `sfscal` and the `fnrm` sequence over ≥ 3 Newton iterations are **bitwise equal to
  serial in every fresh session**.
- **Serial:** bitwise unchanged against `v9-develop`.

**Fallback gate (only by owner decision at the Phase 2 exit, per configuration; decision 9).**
- Bitwise reproducibility across fresh sessions at each thread count.
- Agreement with serial within a tolerance measured and justified in the RCA. For example, a relative bound per
  Jacobian entry and on `fnrm`, set from the observed spread of the configurations that are bitwise.
- The RCA records which configurations use the fallback, and why.

**Functional requirements – Jacobian track**
- ✅ **Before the fix,** the test flow fails on the reproducer: the full problem disagrees with serial in 20/20 runs,
  and the Te box is non-deterministic in ≥ 19/20 runs. Planning measured 7/7 and 10/10. The results are recorded in the
  RCA.
- ✅ **Characterisation** is complete for every configuration on both arms. The open questions on Ti and phi are
  answered.
- ✅ **The RCA Jacobian section** names every root cause and records the Phase 2 exit decision.
- ✅ **After the fix:**
  - agreement holds under the MVP gate (or the agreed fallback) for every configuration and thread count;
  - **0 mismatches in 30/30** fresh-session runs of the Te box and of the full problem at 4 threads, which bounds a
    residual rate below 10 % at 95 % confidence;
  - **0/5** at 2 and 8 threads;
  - **0/5** for each other configuration at 4 threads;
  - checked on Linux and on macOS.
- ✅ **The serial old-vs-new check (§7.3)** passes bitwise on all its cases in both builds. Its output is in every PR
  that touches Fortran, `.v` files or `ppp/`.
- ✅ **`build-test-omp.yml`** gates on the Jacobian arm, passes on the fixed code, and fails in 20/20 runs on the
  pre-fix code and on a seeded race. The seeded race removes `+threadprivate` from a variable written in the
  electron-energy path.
- ✅ **AI-layer code facts updated:** `testing.md`, `openmp-jacobian.md` and `CLAUDE.md` line 38.
  `check_context_refs.py` is clean.

**Functional requirements – Jacobian + RHS track (bonus, does not gate the epic)**
- ✅ The RCA RHS section, with root causes, or a known-limitation statement with evidence.
- ✅ If fixed: the same agreement and acceptance sizes as the Jacobian track, with `OMPParallelPandf1=1`, and the CI RHS
  arm switched to gating.
- ✅ If not fixed: the known limitation is documented (§10), and the CI RHS arm stays report-only.

**Quality indicators (not gating)**
- Agreement at 16 threads.
- OpenMP Jacobian and RHS times before and after the fix.
- A two-X-point case, if one is available.

**User experience goals**
- One command answers "does OpenMP equal serial here, and if not, where?" in under 2 minutes on the Te box.
- A CI failure names the configuration, the arm, and the equation and cell of the first mismatch.
